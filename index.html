Intro

Minghao Zhu 朱铭皓

Physical AI Embodied Intelligence MLLMs Agents

I build multimodal agents that understand dynamic environments, anticipate future states, and act in the physical world.

I am a Ph.D. student at Tongji University, advised by Prof. Chengju Liu. Meanwhile, I am a research intern in the Embodied Intelligence Lab at Alibaba DAMO Academy.
Previously, I received a B.Eng. in Electronic Information Engineering from Tongji University in 2021. I also received a B.Eng. in Computer Engineering from Politecnico di Torino in the same year.
